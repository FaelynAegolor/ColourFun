Chaos: image matrix

Two images for every element and principle in the course design matrix: 120 in all, from paintings, prints, comics, early animation, nature, space, microscopes and chemistry. Some show chaos literally, some conceptually. Click any thumbnail to see it large with the reasons it shows that element and that principle.

The brief

Project 1: Wordscapes · Part 1: Visual Research

  • Find 18 images that represent chaos, literally or conceptually.
  • Each image shows one design principle and one design element.
  • Crop to 6 cm × 6 cm squares, glue onto the grid template and label each.
  • Deliverable: 2 A4 Visual Research sheets.

How to use this page

  • Click a thumbnail to read why it shows its element and principle, then press Choose.
  • Choose 18, ideally from 18 different squares of the matrix, with a mix of literal and conceptual.
  • Print my sheets gives the 2 A4 sheets of 6 cm squares with labels and reasons (print at 100%).
