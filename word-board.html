The brief

Your word

Type your own word if it isn't chaos. Picture ideas are written for chaos; other words get general search ideas.

Task

  • Find 18 pictures that represent the word literally or conceptually.
  • Cut each into a 6 cm × 6 cm square and glue them on the sheet provided.
  • Each picture shows 1 design element and 1 design principle, for example Colour / Emphasis.

Literal or conceptual?

  • Literal: the picture shows the thing itself (a storm, a crowd, a messy room).
  • Conceptual: the picture shows the idea or feeling, or something that behaves like it (TV static, a fractal, a toppled domino run).
  • A mix of both makes a stronger board.

Your 18 squares

Click a square to add a picture (choose a file, paste one, or drag one in from another browser tab), crop it, and pick its element and principle. You can also drop a picture straight onto a square.

    Print

    The squares print at exactly 6 × 6 cm with a thin cutting outline, 12 to an A4 page, each with its number and labels underneath (outside the cut). In the print dialogue choose actual size / 100%, not "fit to page". Check one square with a ruler before cutting them all.
