Chaos: picture list

Pictures that show the word chaos, each matched to one design element and one design principle from the course's design matrix, with the reason it fits and where it comes from. Choose 18, then print them at 6 × 6 cm on two A4 sheets.

The brief

Project 1: Wordscapes · Part 1: Visual Research

  • Find 18 images that represent chaos, literally or conceptually.
  • Each image shows one design principle and one design element.
  • Crop to 6 cm × 6 cm squares without stretching or squashing, glue neatly onto the grid template, and label each with its principle and element.
  • Deliverable: 2 A4 Visual Research sheets.

How to use this page

  • Press Choose on 18 pictures. Your choices are saved in this browser.
  • Try to spread them across the design matrix: different elements and principles, and no pair used twice.
  • Press Print my sheets for the two A4 sheets of 6 cm squares with labels (print at 100%), and Print sources for the list of credits and reasons.

Design matrix

The course matrix: elements across the top, principles down the side. The number in each square is how many pictures in the list show that pair. Green squares are pairs you have chosen. Click a square to show just those pictures.

Click the same square again, or “Show all”, to see every picture.
