Perspective practiser

Perspective makes a flat drawing look deep. Things further away look smaller, and lines going away from you meet at a point. Learn one-, two- and three-point perspective, then practise.

Key words

  • Horizon lineA flat line across the page at your eye level. If you're sitting, it's low; if you're up a ladder, it's high.
  • Vanishing point (VP)A dot on the horizon where lines going away from you meet, like train tracks disappearing into the distance.
  • Guide linesLight ruler lines from the corners of a shape to a vanishing point. Rub them out at the end.
  • VerticalsUpright lines. In one- and two-point perspective they stay straight up and down.
  • Eye levelBelow the horizon you see the tops of things. Above it you see underneath. On it, you see neither.
