For Z

ColourFun

Studio techniques: tools and exercises for colour, form, space and observational drawing.

Colour

Colour schemes

Build a printable worksheet of colour wheel schemes: complementary, analogous, triadic and more.

Colour interaction

Josef Albers-style studies: one colour made to look like two, two colours made to look like one, and more.

Paint mixing

Mix virtual paint, then see tints, tones and shades, and value and saturation. Print mixing charts to fill in.

Form & space

3D shape shading

Turn 3D shapes in a playbox, see them from every side, and learn to draw and shade them with a pencil.

Ellipses

How circles turn into ellipses in perspective, and how their width changes with eye level. For cylinders, bowls, cups and wheels.

Perspective

Learn one-, two- and three-point perspective, move vanishing points around, find them in practice pictures, and print a practice sheet.

Atmospheric perspective

How distance fades contrast, detail and saturation, and pushes colours towards blue. Build a layered landscape.

Reference tools

Value & notan

Turn a reference photo into greyscale, a 2- or 3-value notan, or a 5-value study to plan lights and darks.

Grid & proportion

Put a grid over a reference photo and print a matching grid for your paper, with tools for measuring angles and proportions.

Composition

Put the rule of thirds, golden ratio, golden spiral or dynamic symmetry lines over your own images, and try out crops.

Practice

Design principles

Balance, contrast and hierarchy: the brief, example ideas that regenerate, a box timer, and a printable A4 sheet of six rectangles.

Gesture timer

Timed gesture drawing sessions with your own reference images: 30 seconds to 10 minutes, with breaks and a session log.

Mark-making & media

A reference for pencil grades, charcoal, ink, pastel and paper, with printable swatch sheets to fill in.
