ColourFun

Art tools for learning about colour, shape and shading.

Colour schemes

Build a printable worksheet of colour wheel schemes: complementary, analogous, triadic and more.

3D shape shading

Turn 3D shapes in a playbox, see them from every side, and learn to draw and shade them with a pencil.

Perspective

Learn one-, two- and three-point perspective, play with vanishing points, find them in practice pictures, and print a practice sheet.
